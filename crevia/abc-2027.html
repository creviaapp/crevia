About the event

The old ways of building are breaking down. AI isn't just changing how fast we can work; it is fundamentally rewriting how African businesses operate, scale, and compete.

Following the success of our 1st Edition AI Summit at Strathmore, Crevia is proud to present our 4th annual convening. This year, we are moving away from theoretical discussions and bringing the heavy lifting into the room. This summit is focused on pure operational AI: building resilient infrastructure, automating core business systems, and deploying reality-tested solutions.

Join founders, builders, and investors as we break down the exact blueprints needed to scale African businesses the right way. Less talking. More doing.

What we will cover

  • AI Infrastructure for African businesses
  • Scaling your Business with Automations
  • Using AI as your Co-founder
